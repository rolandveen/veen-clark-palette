# Veen–Clark palette 0.1.0

The first approved palette version, by Roland J. Veen and Charlie J. Clark.
The authoritative definition is `palettes/veen-clark.yaml`.

The palette retains the Okabe–Ito order, lightens sky blue to `#7CC7F2`, darkens
blue to `#005A8D`, and uses yellow `#F5E600`. The other five colours remain
unchanged. Sky blue and blue form the declared `blue_family` for related subgroups.

Included are six language exports, JSON, synthetic plotting examples,
PaletteBench 0.1.0 audit and baseline comparison, full-precision data, provenance,
and a compiled report. The rebuild entry point stages and validates all derived
outputs before replacing them.

The blue pair has greater modelled separation under normal vision and the
reported CVD/grayscale simulations. Some other pairs lose separation, including
blue/black and blue/vermillion under tritan simulation. The yellow choice follows
informal presentation feedback. No controlled participant study has been conducted.

[doi:10.5281/zenodo.23418261](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23418261).
